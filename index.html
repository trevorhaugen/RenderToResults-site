I’m Trevor Haugen. For the past 25 years, I’ve worked professionally as a video producer and creative agency owner, collaborating with hundreds of clients on thousands of projects.

Along the way, my content has helped multiple companies grow into multimillion-dollar businesses. But I haven’t always been able to explain exactly why it worked. Some of that success came through instinct, happy accidents, or luck.

Render to Results is my next chapter: learning how audience, positioning, distribution, and measurement help content work toward a business goal. I want to understand what drives results and make those decisions more deliberately.

I’m documenting the journey as I go. This 100-day experiment still has its own results to earn. That’s why the roadmap is free: it’s the learning plan I’m putting into practice and sharing with you.
